# 🚀 Welcome to n8n Automation Class!

This repository provides your personal, isolated cloud sandbox for learning n8n. You do not need to install anything on your computer. Everything runs directly in your web browser.

---

## ⚡ How to Start Your n8n Environment

Follow these 3 simple steps to launch your personal automation server:

### 1. Launch the Cloud Environment
Click the green **Code** button at the top right of this page.
* Select the **Codespaces** tab.
* Click **Create codespace on main**.

### 2. Wait for the Setup (~60 seconds)
GitHub will build your personal virtual server and configure n8n. You will see a terminal loading screen. **Do not close this tab.**

### 3. Open n8n
* Once the setup finishes, a notification popup will appear in the bottom right corner asking to open Port `5678`. Click **Open in Browser**.
* If you miss the popup, look at the bottom toolbar, click the **Ports** tab, hover over port `5678`, and click the **Globe icon (Open in Browser)**.

---

## 💾 Saving Your Work

* **Automatic Saving:** GitHub Codespaces automatically saves your workflows. 
* **Stopping the Session:** When class finishes, you can simply close the browser tab. Your environment will automatically pause after 30 minutes of inactivity to save your monthly free hours.
* **Returning to Class:** To resume your work tomorrow, go to [://github.com](https://://github.com), find your workspace name, and click it to resume exactly where you left off.

---

## ⚠️ Important Rules for Students
* **Keep the Editor Secret:** Your Codespace URL is unique to you. Do not share your URL with other students, or they will be able to edit your workflows.
* **Do Not Push API Keys to GitHub:** Keep your automation credentials private inside the n8n interface. Do not save them into text files in the main repository folder.
